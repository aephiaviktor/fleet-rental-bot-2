# Fleet Rental Bot 2 v0.1.63

**Bonus Strategy** filters reservable fleets by the active Player Profile’s faction, the configured **Min rate/d**, and demand in the last completed reservation period. A fleet qualifies when at least two distinct player profiles placed successful bids in that period’s final 60 seconds.

The market scan shows demand-check progress. Fleets become eligible for FCFS when the same three checks pass. New listings qualify once they have a completed reservation period with qualifying bids.
