# Fleet Rental Bot 2 v0.1.65

**Bonus Strategy** lists reservable fleets in the active faction at or above **Min rate/d**. Saved FCFS rules can run when their remaining conditions are met.

Use **Check demand** on the visible list or **Check** on an individual fleet. The diagnostic shows the final 60-second bid window, distinct bidder wallets, and Solscan links. It distinguishes a verified zero from incomplete history and RPC errors.
