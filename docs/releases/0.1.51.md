# Fleet Rental Bot 2 v0.1.51

Make the Funding estimate follow each fleet's LCFS setting.

- **LCFS off:** check rent for the complete requested duration only. Exclude the takeover bid and do not show **Bid limit**. Missing or very high takeover bids do not affect the rent-only estimate.
- **LCFS on:** keep checking full rent plus the planned LCFS bid, including the projected expiry floor, against the wallet balance and Max bid.
- Explain rent-only versus rent-plus-bid estimates in the Funding tooltip. A rent-only **Ready** means the rent is funded; it does not establish that a reservation can be acquired.
- Preserve rate-limit warnings, unknown-balance handling, pending-funds deductions, and all execution checks.

For example, Large at 2,900 ATLAS/day for 24 days requires 69,600 ATLAS for the rent-only estimate with LCFS off. Its competing Points bid is excluded. Enabling LCFS restores the takeover-bid requirement and bid-limit warning.

Validation scope: repository, regression tests, and Windows portable release only. No production transaction was submitted and no MUD/ONI/USTUR installation was remotely changed. Wallets, settings, rules, LCFS enablement choices, bidding behavior, launch arguments, and updater behavior are preserved.
