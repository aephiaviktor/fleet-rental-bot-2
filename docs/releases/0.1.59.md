# Fleet Rental Bot 2 v0.1.59

The **ATLAS Earned** page shows **SOL fees (ATLAS)** and **Net profit (ATLAS)** for outbid reservations. The fee includes the original bid transaction’s network fee and recognized tips, converted using Kraken’s prior UTC day closing prices. Net profit subtracts those costs from the earned bonus.

APR now annualizes net profit over the time the ATLAS was locked. Rows with missing fee or price data show a dash for net profit and APR.
