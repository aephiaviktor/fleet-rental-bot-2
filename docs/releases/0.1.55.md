# Fleet Rental Bot 2 v0.1.55

**ATLAS Earned** is available below Rental History. It shows total verified defender bonuses and annualized yield, with a compact table of confirmed outbid reservations. Select a row or its fleet name to open the outbid transaction.

The total uses verified ATLAS bonuses and excludes returned principal. APR uses the bonus over the time the ATLAS bid principal and rent escrow were locked. The combined figure weights each reservation by its locked amount and time, and includes zero-bonus outbids when both are known. Rows with a verified bonus and an unknown holding period or locked amount still contribute to the ATLAS total.

The page uses the configured Player Profile and available transaction history. It shows how many outbid records have verified bonuses and sufficient data for APR. Short historical holding periods can produce large annualized percentages. The figure is historical gross defender yield on the qualifying rows, before SOL fees.
