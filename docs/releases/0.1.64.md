# Fleet Rental Bot 2 v0.1.64

**Bonus Strategy** measures demand using the bidder wallets recorded in confirmed reservation events. A fleet needs successful bids from at least two distinct wallets in the final 60 seconds of its last completed reservation period. Repeat bids from the same wallet count once.

The market list and FCFS eligibility both use the wallet count alongside the active faction and **Min rate/d** filters.
